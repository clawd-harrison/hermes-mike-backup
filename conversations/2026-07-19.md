   - Soham asked about industry shift from loop-based to graph-based multi-agent architectures.
   - **Research:** Extracted Vaibhav Sisinty’s X thread (`https://x.com/VaibhavSisinty/status/2078646016568606961`) and related sources.
   - **Key framing:** *Prompts were the interface → Loops became the workflow → Graphs are becoming the architecture.*
   - **Why graphs:** Single loops fail on long-horizon tasks (hours of runtime, thousands of tool calls) due to spinning out, metric gaming, or incorrect optimization. Graphs distribute oversight across specialized agents (writer, reviewer, breaker, verifier).
   - **Industry validation:** Backed by Boris Cherny (Anthropic), LangGraph runtime, JetBrains, Klarna/Uber production deployments, Kimi K3 Agent Swarm, Andrew Ng commentary.
   - **Caveat:** Astrid Lindholm (@TensorNode) warned that graphs introduce "coordinated drift" via shared state/stale context — robust evals still required.
